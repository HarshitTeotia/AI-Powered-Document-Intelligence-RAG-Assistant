# AI-Powered Document Intelligence & RAG Assistant | Factual handoff

- **Business problem.** Fictional Fresh Distribution has slow order-to-cash, repetitive support, and inaccurate monthly forecasts. The support lead is the proposed pilot owner, with COO sponsorship and IT and finance oversight.
- **Recommended first use case.** Supervised policy assistance for support agents. It ranks 4.25/5 under the provisional impact, ease, data, and risk framework. It can start with approved PDFs and no live ERP integration. Invoice and payment follow-up is second at 3.70/5.
- **Architecture.** Approved PDFs → native extraction or OCR → page-aware passages → local MiniLM embeddings and ChromaDB → semantic search → external Gemini when opted in → validated response and source citations → human review in Streamlit. The model receives the question and relevant excerpts when called.
- **Observed Task 1 results.** 35 questions, 20 answerable, 13 unsupported, and two order-lookup cases. File hits were 20/20 at ranks 1, 3, and 5. Page hits were 14/15, 15/15, and 15/15. Five answerable rows lacked page labels. Page MRR was 0.9556 and required strings appeared for 20/20 at depth five. The mocked replay matched 5/6 valid statuses and verified 4/4 returned source quotes. The previous four live backend checks are historical. Current browser interaction was verified with mocks.
- **Limitations.** Similarity does not establish answerability. Valid citation text can accompany an unsupported claim. The prototype has no live ERP connection or production access controls. Newly measured live answer accuracy and real savings remain unavailable. The four source PDFs require approval for public inclusion.
- **Business case.** The base scenario models 1,200 support queries, half repetitive, and half of repeats safely assisted. Six manual versus three assisted minutes, including review, frees 15 hours per month. Rs. 450/hour and Rs. 2,000 assumed recurring costs yield Rs. 4,750 net monthly staff capacity value. The estimate is illustrative.
- **Leadership action.** Approve discovery, assign owners, and agree a controlled pilot with source approval, a spending ceiling, review gates, and a stop rule for unsafe answers. Move forward only after measured quality and handling time justify it.
