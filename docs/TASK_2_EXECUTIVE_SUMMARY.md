# Fresh Distribution | Leadership decision brief

Fresh Distribution is a fictional distributor with about 400 employees, a limited budget, and no data science team. Its ERP, spreadsheets, and shared inbox support work that still suffers from slow collections, repetitive support queries, and unreliable demand forecasts. The proposed first investment is a supervised policy assistant for support agents. It uses approved PDFs and does not need access to live transactions. Finance follow-up is the next candidate to revisit once receivable and dispute records can be reconciled.

The AI-Powered Document Intelligence & RAG Assistant already demonstrates document lookup, grounded drafting, page citations, and refusal or escalation when the requested information is missing. Its offline retrieval run found the expected file first on 20/20 answerable questions and the right page first on 14/15 labeled questions. This is evidence of search quality on four fictional PDFs. Live customer answer accuracy and business savings have yet to be measured. A valid quotation can accompany an unsupported claim. Agents must review commercial terms before responding, and live order questions stay with authorized staff.

| Scenario | Monthly staff hours released | Net monthly capacity value | Annualized potential capacity value |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Conservative | 3.5 | Rs. 575 | Rs. 6,900 |
| Base | 15.0 | Rs. 4,750 | Rs. 57,000 |
| Optimistic | 34.7 | Rs. 12,100 | Rs. 145,200 |

The base case assumes 1,200 support queries per month, 50% repetitive, and 50% of repeats safely assisted with review. Moving from six to three minutes per assisted case frees 15 hours. At Rs. 450 per hour and Rs. 2,000 of assumed recurring costs, net capacity value is Rs. 4,750 monthly. These are scenario assumptions. The value is released staff capacity rather than a measured cash saving.

**Decision requested** Assign a COO sponsor, support lead, policy and finance reviewers, and IT operator. Approve a one-to-two-week discovery phase to baseline handling time, identify current policies, and decide what may be processed externally. Advance to a two-to-three-week supervised pilot only after approvals, a budget cap and a manual fallback are in place. Measure quality and handling time with review included. Pause on material financial errors or unauthorized data use, and require evidence of benefit before funding MVP. [Full consulting case study](TASK_2_CLIENT_CONSULTING_CASE_STUDY.md).
